Lets Deklutter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lets Deklutter ("we", "our", "us"). We are a free, volunteer-run community platform dedicated to helping people rehome pre-owned items across India. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application ("App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Item listings: Title, description, price, category, city, and photos you upload when posting an item.
  • Contact details (optional): Your WhatsApp phone number, if you choose to provide it so buyers can contact you.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics such as app interactions, collected automatically by Firebase Analytics.

We do not collect payment information, location tracking, or device contacts.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your item listings to other users in the community.
  • To allow buyers to contact you via WhatsApp or email about your listings.
  • To improve the App experience and fix bugs.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage), which is hosted on Google's infrastructure. Photos are stored in Firebase Storage and item data in Cloud Firestore. We use Firebase Authentication to verify your identity.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase – authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Google Sign-In – account authentication.
  • WhatsApp – used only when a buyer taps "Chat on WhatsApp" to contact a seller. The App opens WhatsApp externally; we do not access your WhatsApp messages.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Delete your listings at any time from within the App.
  • Sign out of your account at any time via the Profile screen.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
